\section{Data Flow Summary}

\[
\underbrace{\text{Dermoscopy image}}_{\text{input}}
\;\xrightarrow{\text{Vertex AI ViT}}\;
\underbrace{\mathbf{e} \in \mathbb{R}^{1408}}_{\text{embedding}}
\;\xrightarrow{\text{MLP}}\;
\underbrace{\mathbf{p} \in \Delta^5}_{\text{6-class softmax}}
\;\xrightarrow[\text{clinical + cohort}]{\text{concat}}\;
\underbrace{\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{13}}_{\text{feature vec}}
\;\xrightarrow{\text{XGBoost}}\;
\underbrace{\hat{y} \in [0,1]}_{\substack{P(\text{malignant})}}
\;\xrightarrow{\tau = 0.21}\;
\underbrace{\{0,1\}}_{\text{decision}}
\]

\vspace{0.5em}

where $\Delta^5$ denotes the 6-dimensional probability simplex
($\sum_{c} p_c = 1,\; p_c \geq 0$),
and $\tau$ is the tuned malignancy threshold achieving
recall $= 0.958 \geq 0.95$ on the held-out test set.

\vspace{1em}

\section{Key Design Decisions}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} >{\bfseries\small}p{4cm} X @{}}
\toprule
Decision & Rationale \\
\midrule
MLP before XGBoost
  & Compresses 1408-D embedding to 6-D class probability vector.
    XGBoost then combines this with clinical metadata and cohort scores.
    The MLP acts as a domain-adapted feature extractor. \\
\addlinespace
OOF discipline (Stages 3--4)
  & Both MLP softmax outputs and XGBoost threshold are derived from
    out-of-fold predictions — data the model never trained on.
    Eliminates data leakage between the two stages. \\
\addlinespace
Benign-only KNN baseline
  & The ``ugly duckling'' sign: a lesion that looks different from
    benign lesions in the same demographic cohort is suspicious.
    Including malignant cases in the baseline would contaminate the signal. \\
\addlinespace
Bayesian update post-XGBoost
  & Physician adjusts clinical context (age, sex, site) interactively.
    Re-running XGBoost per adjustment would be slow and unnecessary;
    a precomputed prior table is sufficient and instant. \\
\addlinespace
Threshold $= 0.21$ (not 0.5)
  & Clinical target is malignant recall $\geq 0.95$.
    Default threshold 0.5 yields recall $= 0.87$ (misses 13\% of cancers).
    Tuned threshold of 0.21 accepts more false positives in exchange
    for catching 95.8\% of malignant cases. \\
\addlinespace
Scale\_pos\_weight $= 2.24$
  & Training set has 2.24 benign cases per malignant case.
    XGBoost's built-in weight corrects the gradient without SMOTE,
    which would be inappropriate on derived probability features. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
